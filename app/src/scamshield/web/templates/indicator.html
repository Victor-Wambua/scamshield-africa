How this is worked out

Starts at 10%. Adds 12% per independent reporter (up to 5), 8% per evidence item (up to 3), 20% when a moderator attached primary evidence and 12% once verified. Capped at 40% while a correction request is open. It measures how well supported the report is, not how likely fraud is. Raw evidence and reporter identity are never shown publicly.

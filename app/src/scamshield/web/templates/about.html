How we label things

What this is

ScamShield Africa records what people report about phone numbers, links, emails, wallets and messages. It is not a public accusation list and never publishes names of private individuals.

Statuses

Verified with evidence
A moderator confirmed it AND either at least {{ settings.verify_min_independent }} independent reporters (different accounts and different devices) support it, or a moderator attached primary evidence.
Reported, not verified
Accepted after review, but not enough evidence to verify.
Disputed, under review
Someone asked for a correction. Wording is softened until decided.

Repeat reports from one person count once. Not reported does not mean safe.

Privacy notice (pilot summary)

Reports are stored securely. Screenshots and message text are visible only to you, moderators and, for reports an institution submitted itself, that institution. Public pages never show evidence or who reported. Phone numbers and emails are partly hidden in lists; an exact search shows the full indicator. You can ask for your data to be exported or your account deleted in Settings. This pilot has not been legally reviewed and makes no compliance claim.

Data origin

Every record carries an origin label: user-submitted, synthetic test data, mock integration, officially published or verified live. Demonstration data is labelled in the banner at the top of each page.

# Pilot guide (Kenya, limited institutional pilot; nothing is live)

**Prerequisites before any pilot:** native Swahili review, legal review (Data Protection Act 2019 considerations, takedown process), a real mail provider, a staging deployment with PostgreSQL, a trained moderator rota, signed partner terms. None exist yet.
**Onboarding an institution:** platform admin approves the organisation (`/admin/orgs`), invites an org admin (one-time link), who enrols TOTP, creates a test API key, invites analysts, sets alert rules. Start with 1 bank or mobile-money provider and 1 NGO.
**Support workflow:** support mailbox -> triage within 1 business day -> corrections follow the 7-day SLA (urgent legal 1 day) -> incidents logged in the audit trail -> weekly review of moderation queue age.
**Success metrics:** reports per week; share reaching a moderator decision within 3 days; median time to first decision; independent-reports-per-verified-indicator; verified indicators per week; lookup volume; correction requests per 100 records and share accepted (accuracy proxy); partner weekly active analysts; API calls and error rate; false-positive complaints.
**Impact metrics (to measure, none claimed):** lookups made before a payment (survey), reports that led to partner action (cases opened/closed), time from first report to verified warning, reduction in repeat reports on blocked indicators.
**Feedback:** in-product link to a feedback form (not built), fortnightly partner call, moderator retro.
**Risk register:** (1) defamation/wrong accusation - masking, no private-person names, corrections SLA, evidence thresholds; (2) abuse/brigading - independence rule, restrictions, audit; (3) moderator overload - queue KPIs, caps; (4) data breach - MFA, tenant isolation, encryption in transit, restricted evidence; (5) false sense of safety - "not reported does not mean safe" everywhere; (6) analysis misses - disclaimer, rules transparent; (7) partner data misuse - scoped keys, export logging; (8) regulatory change - legal review.
**Business model:** free public reporting and lookup; paid institutional dashboards (per org per month); paid API tiers (calls/month); enterprise threat-intel feed; sponsored consumer-protection deployments. Prices are not set and no revenue is claimed.
